Every creation that changed the course of humanity had a creator who went All In

From Elon Musk betting his last dollar on Tesla to the Wright brothers flying in obscurity for six years before the world took notice. Building something truly original and world-changing has always required the builder to go All In.

Kushal Bhagia

Founding Partners, All In Capital

Kushal is the founder of All In Capital. He's been working with tech startups - either as a builder or as an investor since 2010 when he launched his first company from his BITS Goa hostel room. He subsequently founded Nayi Disha Studios - a gaming company that built educational games for kindergarteners, worked with upGrad as their first BD hire, and built a pre-seed fund called First Cheque where he invested in 100+ startups via a network of some of India's best founder angels. Three years of working with founders at the earliest stages made him feel 'founder-market-fit' for the first time in his life and he decided to go All In and launch his own fund!.

Apart from helping build companies and backing founders, Kushal loves reading books, broadcasting his thoughts on Twitter, and heading back to the beaches of Goa every chance he gets! You can reach him on kb@allincapital.vc!

Aditya Singh

Founding Partners

Aditya is the co-founder of All In Capital. He started his journey in the startup ecosystem in 2012 as an entrepreneur. Over nine years, Aditya wore multiple hats, built India's first forex marketplace Bookmyforex.com (acquired by MakeMyTrip), did startup BD at Amazon, and angel invested in 50+ companies. Before All In Capital, he was founding principal at Stride Ventures India's leading venture debt fund with $150M in AUM.

Apart from working with startups, Aditya loves playing sports and hitting the gym, he is a certified Crossfit trainer. You can reach him on as@allincapital.vc!

Disha Choudhary

Analyst

Ritvik Sharma

Vice President

Ritvik joined All In Capital in 2024. Prior to joining All In, he worked with IIMA Ventures in their deep tech and fintech teams. He started his career as a growth and product manager with 0-1 stage startups.

All In Founder Stories

Magma One of the first funds to take a bet on Magma in our first round

All In Capital was one of the first funds to take a bet on Magma in our first round. I started the company after I graduated from university and I was 21 years old when All In first backed me.

After raising my Preseed, I refined the business model to focus on a more strategic direction, and they were incredibly patient and supportive in helping me navigate the nuances of building a B2B marketplace.

When we hit some scale on the new direction we decided to go out and raise a seed round. While I had a strong understanding of the manufacturing supply chains I began with — starting in ceramics and expanding into other customer categories — raising capital from VCs was a steep learning curve.

Through this journey, Kushal and Aditya thoroughly coached me on how to pitch to VCs and gave me invaluable advice in hiring and managing expectations of investors. Not only that - they went out of their way to introduce me to a plethora of VCs from India and abroad in their network so much so that all of the investors who ended up investing in our seed round were introduced to us by All In Capital.

All In Capital is one the best funds for first time founders. The entire team is one call away and will do whatever they can to solve your problems.

- Neal Thakker, Founder and CEO of Magma
